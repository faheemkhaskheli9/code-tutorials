# Installing the Quill 2

Mount the unit 2.0 m above ground level on the supplied pole bracket, at least
3 m from any wall, fence or tree canopy. The radiation shield must face north
in the northern hemisphere and south in the southern hemisphere.

Use the bubble level moulded into the bracket: the unit has to sit within 2
degrees of vertical or the rain gauge under-reports.

Insert a microSD card of up to 128 GB, formatted FAT32, before powering the
unit on. A card larger than 128 GB, or one formatted exFAT, is rejected at
boot with a red LED.

First boot takes about 40 seconds: the unit writes a fresh `quill.cfg` to the
card, acquires the radio channel and then runs a self-test of all four
sensors. Wait for a single green flash before you close the enclosure.

To pair the unit with a base station, hold the pairing button for 5 seconds
within 30 minutes of the base station being powered on. Pairing is one to one;
a base station accepts a single Quill 2.
